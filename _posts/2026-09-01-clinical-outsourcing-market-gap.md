The clinical outsourcing market is growing fast — the global CRO market is on track to reach roughly $92B by 2030, and the FSP segment alone is projected to nearly double to ~$33B by 2032. But that growth hasn't closed the actual gap many organizations live with day to day.

Small-to-mid biotechs — who are behind an estimated 40% of new FDA drug approvals — often can't justify a full-time clinical development executive, yet still need senior-level oversight to get a program investor-ready.

Consultancies and CROs running FSP models frequently need flexible surge capacity or regional delivery reach (particularly across Southeast Europe/CEE) that's slow and expensive to build internally.
