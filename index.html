About

The experience behind the advice.

Traiche Nushev, M.D.
Founder & Principal Consultant

20+ years in clinical research and operations across CRO and pharma, including 17+ years in oncology and onco-hematology and 9+ years in Phase I and early Phase II studies, with global roles at Novartis, IQVIA and WCG and CRO leadership positions across Europe.

I led the launch and clinical-data implementation of the first Entresto Post-Approval Study in the US & Canada within Novartis' PLS Cardiology program, and built the clinical operations function for Bulgaria & Serbia at Accelsiors CRO, including direct FDA audit readiness. Over two decades I've led 600+ monitoring visits and 200+ site initiation visits across Phase I–IV, from oncology and cardiovascular to immunology, rare disease and CAR-T/first-in-human programs.

I hold an MD from the Medical University of Sofia and completed immunology coursework at Harvard Medical School in 2025. Today I bring that depth to small-to-mid-sized biotechs as fractional leadership.
