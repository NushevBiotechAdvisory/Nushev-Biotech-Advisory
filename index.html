Traiche Nushev, MD

Founder & Principal Consultant

20+ years in clinical research and operations across CRO and pharma environments, including 17+ years in oncology/onco-hematology and 9+ years in Phase I/early Phase II studies. My background spans global roles at organizations including Novartis, IQVIA, and WCG, along with CRO leadership positions across Europe.

I led the launch and clinical-data implementation of the first Entresto Post-Approval Study in the US & Canada, a milestone within Novartis' PLS Cardiology program, and built the clinical operations function for Bulgaria & Serbia at Accelsiors CRO, including direct FDA audit readiness. Over two decades I've executed 600+ routine monitoring visits and 200+ site initiation visits across Phase I–IV trials, spanning oncology, cardiovascular, immunology, rare disease, and CAR-T/first-in-human programs.

I hold an MD from the Medical University of Sofia and completed immunology coursework at Harvard Medical School in 2025. I now bring that operational and clinical depth to small-to-mid-sized biotechs as fractional leadership — without the cost of a full-time C-level hire.
